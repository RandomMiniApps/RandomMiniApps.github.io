What ScamLens does

ScamLens gives you a calm second opinion before you tap, pay, or reply. It reviews links, QR codes, pasted messages and screenshots using a rule engine and on-device intelligence, then shows a clear risk level with the reasons in plain English.

Built for the UK

ScamLens knows the scams Britons actually receive: parcel redelivery fees, HMRC "refunds", bank impersonation and safe-account fraud, parking QR stickers, Winter Fuel Payment texts, eVisa fee demands, marketplace courier tricks, and more — with the correct current UK reporting route for each.

Scam text? Forward it free to 7726. Suspicious email? Send it to report@phishing.gov.uk. A call about your bank account? Hang up and dial 159. Lost money? Report to Report Fraud (reportfraud.police.uk) — or Police Scotland on 101.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ScamLens UK?

ScamLens UK is an on-device educational scam checker for iPhone. It reviews suspicious links, QR codes, messages and screenshots and explains warning signs in plain English before you tap, pay or reply.

Does it upload my messages?

No. Checks run entirely on your iPhone. Messages, links, screenshots and results are never uploaded, transmitted or logged. Our App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Can it guarantee a link is safe?

No. ScamLens is an educational safety tool. A "Low risk" result means no obvious warning signs were found — it is not a promise of safety. Always verify through official channels before sharing personal or payment details.

What is free?

Five checks per day, QR destination previews, verdicts, safer next steps, UK reporting guidance and the full scam library are free. Pro adds convenience features like unlimited checks, screenshot analysis, full history and Helper mode.

What permissions does it need?

Camera is used only while you scan a QR code — nothing is recorded or uploaded. Photos access is via the system picker for screenshot checking; images are processed on-device and never stored unless you save a check to history.

Who is ScamLens for?

Anyone in the UK who receives suspicious texts, emails, QR codes or links — and anyone helping family members who need a calm, plain-English second opinion before they tap.

Important notes

ScamLens is an educational safety tool. It helps you spot common scam warning signs but cannot guarantee that any link, QR code, message or website is safe. HMRC, Royal Mail, Evri, DVLA, NHS, bank names and other organisations mentioned are referenced for identification only — ScamLens is independent and not affiliated with them.

Reporting shortcuts connect you to third-party services we do not operate. You remain responsible for your own decisions, including any link you choose to open.
