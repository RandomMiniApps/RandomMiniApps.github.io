What ScamLens is

ScamLens UK is an educational safety tool. It looks for known warning signs in links, QR codes, messages and screenshots — impersonation of familiar organisations, look-alike addresses, hidden destinations, pressure tactics and wording used in common UK scams — and explains what it finds in plain English.

What ScamLens is not

ScamLens cannot guarantee that any link, QR code, message or website is safe, and cannot detect every scam. A "Low risk" result means no obvious warning signs were found; it is not a promise of safety. Results are risk assessments to inform your own judgement, not professional, legal or financial advice. You remain responsible for your decisions, including any link you choose to open and any information you choose to share.
