Dyslexia Reading Lens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy describes how Dyslexia Reading Lens handles information. Your documents and AI processing stay on your device.

Stored on your device

  • Documents you paste, scan, import, or share into the app
  • Reading preferences, folders, tags, and progress
  • AI output you choose to save with a document

Delete everything from Profile → Privacy → Delete all my data.

On-device AI

  • All AI tools — simplify, summarise, explain words, quiz, and more — run on your device
  • Simplify and summarise use Apple Intelligence on-device when available
  • Your document text is not sent to our servers for AI processing
  • AI responses may be inaccurate — verify important information

On your device only

  • Document scanning and OCR (Apple Vision)
  • Text-to-speech and reading presentation
  • Local library storage

Uses the internet

  • Subscriptions — purchases and restore are handled by Apple
  • Analytics — optional anonymous usage events (no document contents)

No account required

Version 1.0 does not require a user account. We do not collect your name or email through the app.

AI disclaimer

AI responses may be inaccurate. Verify important information. The app does not diagnose or treat dyslexia or any medical condition.
