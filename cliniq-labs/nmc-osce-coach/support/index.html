About the app

What is NMC OSCE AI Coach?

NMC OSCE AI Coach is an independent OSCE-style voice practice app for UK adult nursing exam preparation.

Is NMC OSCE AI Coach affiliated with the NMC?

No. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by the Nursing and Midwifery Council.

Does the app provide official OSCE marking?

No. Feedback and estimated outcomes are local educational practice only and are not official OSCE marking.

Is the clinical content medical advice?

No. Scenarios and feedback are for exam communication practice only and must not be used for real patient care.

Who is NMC OSCE AI Coach for?

It is designed for internationally educated nurses preparing for UK adult nursing OSCE-style communication practice.

Does the app guarantee exam success?

No. The app does not guarantee any exam result, pass, or registration outcome.
