NMC OSCE AI Coach

Features and intended use

Features, intended use and limitations for NMC OSCE AI Coach.

What NMC OSCE AI Coach does

NMC OSCE AI Coach is an independent educational practice tool for internationally educated nurses preparing for UK adult nursing OSCE-style communication exams.

It provides original practice stations with timed flow, voice interaction, transcripts and checklist-style feedback. It does not provide official OSCE marking or guarantee any exam result.

Who is NMC OSCE AI Coach for?

NMC OSCE AI Coach is designed for nurses who want focused, repeatable OSCE-style voice practice on iPhone and iPad.

  • Internationally educated nurses preparing for UK registration
  • Adult nursing candidates practising OSCE communication stations
  • Learners who want timed door-note and station flow
  • Candidates building confidence speaking aloud under exam pressure
  • Busy students fitting short practice sessions into a study plan
  • Nurses who want structured feedback and weakness tracking

Key features

Timed OSCE-style stations

Practise adult nursing stations with door notes, preparation time and timed station flow.

AI patient voice practice

Speak aloud with patient and examiner-style voice interaction during practice.

Checklist feedback

Review transcript capture, checklist-style feedback and missed safety-point detection.

Plans, viva rescue and phrase bank

Use weakness tracking, 7-day and 14-day exam plans, viva rescue drills and a UK clinical communication phrase bank.

How NMC OSCE AI Coach works

  1. Choose an adult nursing practice station.
  2. Read the door note and use the preparation timer.
  3. Complete the timed station aloud with voice interaction.
  4. Review your transcript and checklist-style feedback.
  5. Track weaknesses, follow an exam plan and practise again.

Useful for

NMC OSCE AI Coach supports common OSCE preparation needs for adult nursing candidates.

  • OSCE station communication flow
  • Patient interaction and reassurance
  • Safety-critical communication points
  • Viva rescue practice
  • Transcript review after attempts
  • Structured checklist feedback
  • 7-day and 14-day study plans
  • UK clinical phrase practice

Why use NMC OSCE AI Coach?

OSCE preparation often needs repeated spoken practice under time pressure, not just reading notes. NMC OSCE AI Coach gives nurses a private, repeatable way to rehearse exam-style communication.

The app is a preparation tool and works best alongside official NMC preparation materials, university guidance and real-world speaking practice.

Important notes

NMC OSCE AI Coach is an independent educational practice tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by the Nursing and Midwifery Council. Always use official NMC preparation materials and guidance when preparing for your exam.

Scenarios and feedback are for exam communication practice only. They are not clinical guidance and must not be used for real patient care. Practice results shown in the app are local educational feedback only and do not guarantee any exam outcome.
