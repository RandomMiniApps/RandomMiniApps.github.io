About the app

What is MedViva Coach?

MedViva Coach is an AI-powered mock interview app that helps aspiring medical students practise medicine interview-style questions aloud and receive structured feedback.

Is MedViva Coach for UK medicine applicants?

Yes. MedViva Coach is especially designed for A-level students and UK medical school applicants preparing for medicine interviews.

Can I practise MMI-style questions with MedViva Coach?

MedViva Coach can help students practise common medicine interview themes, including MMI-style communication, reflection, ethical reasoning and motivation questions.

Does MedViva Coach give feedback on my answers?

Yes. The app gives structured feedback to help students improve their communication, confidence, clarity, reflection and answer structure.

Is MedViva Coach a replacement for a tutor?

No. MedViva Coach is a practice tool. It can support interview preparation, but it should not replace teachers, tutors, mentors, official university guidance or real mock interview practice.

Who should use MedViva Coach?

MedViva Coach is suitable for A-level students, aspiring medical students, UK medicine applicants, and students who want to improve their confidence before medical school interviews.
