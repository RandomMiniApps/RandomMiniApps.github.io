Features and intended use

Features, intended use and limitations for Dyslexia Reading Lens.

What Dyslexia Reading Lens does

Dyslexia Reading Lens is a personal reading and comprehension workspace for people who find text visually overwhelming, tiring, or hard to understand. It is practical and respectful — designed to support reading comfort, not to claim a cure for dyslexia.

Paste text, scan printed pages, or import PDFs and images into a local library. Open any document in a dyslexia-friendly reader with focus tools, listen with text-to-speech highlighting, and use AI to simplify, summarise, or explain difficult words when you need help.

Who is Dyslexia Reading Lens for?

The app is built for learners and adults who want scan, read, listen, and AI comprehension in one calm place instead of juggling several apps.

  • University and sixth-form students with dyslexia
  • Adults managing emails, PDFs, and work documents
  • Parents supporting children with reading difficulties
  • People with ADHD or reading fatigue
  • Non-native English readers working through dense text
  • Anyone who prefers listening while following highlighted text

Key features

Comfortable reading view

Adjust fonts, spacing, themes, focus line, and chunk mode. Reading profiles remember what works for you.

Listen with highlighting

Text-to-speech follows the words on screen so you can read and listen at the same pace.

Scan and import documents

Camera OCR for printed pages, plus PDF, text, and image import into your on-device library.

AI comprehension tools

Simplify dense passages, summarise long readings, and explain words you are stuck on.

How Dyslexia Reading Lens works

  1. Add text by pasting, scanning a page, or importing a file.
  2. Open the document and tune your reading style in one tap.
  3. Read with focus tools or tap Listen to hear it aloud.
  4. Use Simplify, Summarise, or Explain when comprehension gets hard.
  5. Save progress in your library and pick up where you left off.

Useful for

Dyslexia Reading Lens supports everyday reading tasks that usually need multiple separate tools.

  • Assignment briefs copied from the web
  • Printed worksheets and handouts
  • Lecture slides and PDF readings
  • Long emails and work documents
  • Letters, forms, and official paperwork
  • Revising with read-aloud and shorter summaries
  • Organising documents with folders and tags
  • Sharing text in through the iOS share sheet

Why use Dyslexia Reading Lens?

Many readers stitch together a scanner app, a customisable reader, a TTS app, and an AI tool. Dyslexia Reading Lens brings those steps into one focused iOS workspace with privacy-first defaults: your library and AI processing stay on your device.

The free tier is genuinely useful for trying the core loop. Pro subscriptions unlock higher limits for scanning, listening, and AI when you need them every day.

Important notes

Dyslexia Reading Lens is designed to support reading comfort and comprehension. It does not guarantee specific educational outcomes. Fonts and display settings are personal preferences, not clinical treatments.

AI tools run on your device. See the app-specific privacy policy for details of data handling. You can delete all app data from Profile → Privacy.
