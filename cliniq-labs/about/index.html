About the maker

Independent by design.
Personal by nature.

Cliniq Labs and Logiq Labs bring a clearer focus to apps previously presented under Random Mini Apps.

Built by Hassan Chizari

These are independent apps built by Hassan Chizari. The two brands organise the collection around the people and purposes each app serves. They do not represent separate clinical institutions or imply endorsement by an exam body.

Two homes, a shared approach

Cliniq Labs brings together healthcare education and preparation tools. Its research projects are described separately, with their current limitations.

Logiq Labs brings together reading tools, digital confidence and practical everyday apps. Each product has its own purpose, feature details and privacy information.

Clarity before claims

We want you to understand what an app does before downloading it. Product pages explain its intended use, limitations and where to get help. Illustrative website examples are labelled, and are not presented as actual app screenshots or customer results.

A familiar developer name

You may still see Random Mini Apps or the developer’s name in earlier app versions, privacy notices or store information. The new brands provide a clearer home for the existing collection.
