Features, intended use and limitations for UCAT Sprint Coach.

What UCAT Sprint Coach does

UCAT Sprint Coach is an independent preparation tool for medicine and dentistry applicants practising Verbal Reasoning, Decision Making, Quantitative Reasoning and Situational Judgement.

It includes original practice items, timed mini-drills, a full mock workflow with official-style subtest timing, explanations, a review notebook and local progress analytics. Estimated scores are practice guidance only.

Who is UCAT Sprint Coach for?

UCAT Sprint Coach is designed for students who want focused, repeatable UCAT-style practice on Apple devices.

  • Medicine and dentistry applicants preparing for the UCAT
  • Students who want timed drills that fit short study sessions
  • Candidates practising full mock exam workflow
  • Learners who prefer privacy-first apps with no account
  • Users who want progress trends and study recommendations
  • Students who need dyslexia-friendly reading options

Key features

1,444 original questions

Practice across VR, DM, QR and SJT with explanations after each question.

Timed drills and full mocks

Use untimed learning, timed mini-drills and full mock flow with flagging, navigator and calculator tools.

Progress and study planning

Track accuracy trends, readiness guidance, subtest balance and next-practice recommendations.

Privacy-first by design

Works offline after install. Practice data stays on your device. No account and no tracking SDKs.

How UCAT Sprint Coach works

  1. Choose a subtest and practice mode (untimed, timed drill or mock).
  2. Answer questions with exam-style tools such as flagging, navigator and calculator where relevant.
  3. Review explanations and save incorrect or flagged items to your notebook.
  4. Check your dashboard for accuracy trends and readiness guidance.
  5. Follow study recommendations and official UCAT cycle dates as you prepare.

Useful for

UCAT Sprint Coach supports common preparation needs for medicine and dentistry applicants.

  • Verbal Reasoning passage practice
  • Decision Making logic and multi-statement items
  • Quantitative Reasoning tables and charts
  • Situational Judgement scenarios
  • Timed mini-drills under exam pressure
  • Full mock exam workflow
  • Review of incorrect and flagged questions
  • Local study planning and progress tracking

Why use UCAT Sprint Coach?

UCAT preparation benefits from repeated, timed practice with clear feedback — not just reading theory. UCAT Sprint Coach gives students a private, offline-friendly way to rehearse exam-style workflow on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

The app is a preparation tool and works best alongside official UCAT preparation resources and guidance from the UCAT Consortium.

Important notes

UCAT Sprint Coach is an independent preparation tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the UCAT Consortium, Pearson VUE, the General Medical Council, the Medical Schools Council, Apple, or any university. UCAT is used only to describe the examination for which users may be preparing.

All practice questions in this app are original unless otherwise stated. Estimated scores are for practice guidance only and are not official UCAT scores. Always use official UCAT preparation materials when preparing for your exam.
