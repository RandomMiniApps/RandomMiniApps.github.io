What is UCAT Sprint Coach?

UCAT Sprint Coach is an independent UCAT preparation app with original practice questions, timed drills, full mocks and progress analytics for medicine and dentistry applicants.

Is UCAT Sprint Coach affiliated with the UCAT Consortium?

No. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the UCAT Consortium, Pearson VUE, the GMC, the Medical Schools Council, or any university.

Are the practice questions official UCAT questions?

No. All practice questions are original UCAT-style items created for this app. They are not official UCAT questions.

Does the app give official UCAT scores?

No. Estimated scores and readiness metrics are for practice guidance only and are not official UCAT scores.

Is an account required?

No. Practice data stays on your device. No account is required.

Is there a free version?

Yes. The free tier includes 50 questions and one diagnostic mini-drill. Premium unlocks the full bank, mocks, analytics and study plan via subscription or lifetime purchase.
