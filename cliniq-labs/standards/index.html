How to understand the scope of Cliniq Labs education apps and research projects.

Independent education

Our preparation apps offer practice and reflection. They are not official exam products, do not provide official scores and do not guarantee a pass, registration or admission. Check the relevant official body for current requirements.

Feedback is a starting point

AI-generated feedback may be incomplete or inaccurate. Use it as a prompt for reflection alongside official guidance, educators and real-world practice. Fictional scenarios are not clinical advice or instructions for patient care.

Be specific about evidence

A product description is not proof of clinical validation. We do not present website examples as measured outcomes or independent reviews. Any future reviewer contribution should be described by its actual scope and date.

Research has a separate purpose

Cardiac Aftercare and FractureFlow AI are invite-only research prototypes. Their public descriptions do not authorise patient use, live clinical decisions or public distribution.

Questions or corrections

If you spot unclear wording or a problem with a practice example, tell us which app and version you are using and describe the issue without including patient information.
