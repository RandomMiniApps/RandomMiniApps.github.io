Car HUD Display

The details,
in plain English.

Features, intended use and limitations for Car HUD Display.

What Car HUD Display does

Car HUD Display projects essential driving information in a clear landscape interface designed for windshield reflection or direct in-car viewing.

The app combines real-time GPS speed with configurable widgets including clock, temperature, wind, compass, bearing, altitude, trip distance, average speed, max speed, trip time and battery level.

Who is Car HUD Display for?

Car HUD Display is designed for drivers who want essential trip and speed information in a simple, high-contrast interface.

  • Daily commuters
  • Road trip drivers
  • Drivers who prefer HUD-style displays
  • People who want speed and trip data at a glance
  • iPhone users who want a dedicated driving display
  • Drivers who need imperial and metric unit support

Key features

Large GPS speed display

A prominent speedometer shows real-time GPS speed in MPH or km/h.

Custom widget system

Choose up to four widgets from speed, clock, temperature, compass, trip and device data.

HUD mirror mode

Mirror the display for windshield reflection and use full brightness when needed.

Smart brightness

The app can adjust brightness using ambient camera readings for better visibility.

How Car HUD Display works

  1. Open the app before starting your trip.
  2. Choose your preferred units and widgets.
  3. Place the iPhone safely for direct viewing or windshield reflection.
  4. Monitor speed, heading, weather and trip data while driving.
  5. Reset trip tracking when you want a fresh journey summary.

Useful for

Car HUD Display is useful in common driving situations where fast, readable trip data matters.

  • Speed monitoring
  • Road trips
  • Commuting
  • Trip distance tracking
  • Compass and bearing checks
  • Windshield reflection mode
  • High-contrast night driving display
  • Metric and imperial driving data

Why use Car HUD Display?

Car HUD Display keeps important driving data visible in one focused interface, reducing the need to switch between multiple apps or screens.

It is designed as a utility for glanceable information. Drivers should always follow local laws, mount devices safely and keep attention on the road.

Important notes

Car HUD Display requires location access for speed and GPS information. Weather data depends on network availability.

Use the app only when it is safe and legal to do so. Secure your device properly and do not interact with the app while driving.
