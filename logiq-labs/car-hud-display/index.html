What is Car HUD Display?

Car HUD Display is an iPhone app that shows GPS speed, trip information and configurable widgets in a heads-up display style.

Does Car HUD Display support MPH and km/h?

Yes. The app supports both Imperial and Metric units.

Can I use Car HUD Display on my windshield?

Yes. The app includes a mirror display mode designed for windshield reflection.

What widgets are available?

Widgets include clocks, temperature, wind speed, compass, bearing, altitude, trip distance, average speed, max speed, trip time and battery level.

Does Car HUD Display require location access?

Yes. Location access is required for accurate GPS speed and trip information.

Is Car HUD Display a replacement for my car dashboard?

No. It is an auxiliary display tool. Always rely on your vehicle instruments and drive safely.
