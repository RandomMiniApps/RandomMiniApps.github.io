What is MsgSecret?

MsgSecret is an app for encoding and decoding messages with classic cipher methods.

Can I send encoded text through other apps?

Yes. You can copy ciphertext from MsgSecret and paste it into email, SMS, chat or social apps.

Does the recipient need MsgSecret?

To decode the message easily, the recipient needs MsgSecret and the same cipher settings.

Does MsgSecret encrypt on device?

Yes. The text transformation happens on device.

Is MsgSecret free?

Yes. MsgSecret is free to use.

Is MsgSecret a replacement for secure messaging?

No. It is a cipher and encoding utility, not a full secure messaging system.
