Features, intended use and limitations for MsgSecret.

What MsgSecret does

MsgSecret helps keep the meaning of your messages out of plain form before the text leaves your device. You set up your cipher, encrypt on device and share ciphertext through any app or pasteboard.

Separate plaintext and ciphertext fields make it easier to switch between encoding and decoding without overwriting your last step.

Who is MsgSecret for?

MsgSecret is for people who want a simple cipher-based way to encode and decode text.

  • People sharing encoded messages
  • Users who want classic cipher tools
  • Students learning about ciphers
  • People sending ciphertext through messaging apps
  • Users who want a focused encode/decode utility
  • iPhone users who prefer on-device text transformation

Key features

Encode and decode text

Transform plaintext into ciphertext and decode incoming text in one focused app.

Multiple classic ciphers

Choose from different methods with explanations so setup stays straightforward.

Share anywhere

Copy ciphertext to email, SMS, WhatsApp, social apps or any text field.

Tutorial and cipher reference

A built-in tutorial and About ciphers section help users get started.

How MsgSecret works

  1. Choose whether to encrypt or decrypt.
  2. Enter your text and cipher settings.
  3. Run the transformation on device.
  4. Copy the result into email, SMS, chat or social apps.
  5. Decode incoming ciphertext with MsgSecret and the same settings.

Useful for

MsgSecret supports everyday text encoding and cipher learning use cases.

  • Encoding messages
  • Decoding incoming ciphertext
  • Classic cipher exploration
  • Copy and paste workflows
  • Email and SMS sharing
  • Chat and social app sharing
  • Tutorial-guided setup
  • On-device text transformation

Why use MsgSecret?

MsgSecret reduces what service providers and platforms can infer from message text alone because they carry ciphertext instead of the original wording.

It is a cipher utility, not a guarantee of military-grade or end-to-end security. Use it for privacy-minded encoding and learning, not for high-risk secrets.

Important notes

MsgSecret is a text encoding and cipher utility. It should not be treated as a replacement for audited secure messaging or professional security tools.

Recipients need the correct method and settings to decode the text.
