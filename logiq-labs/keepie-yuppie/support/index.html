Before you contact us

I need help with a purchase

About the app

What is Keepie Yuppie?

Keepie Yuppie is a physics-based ball juggling game for iPhone and iPad.

How many levels does Keepie Yuppie have?

The game includes 99 levels.

What weather effects are included?

The game includes dynamic weather such as wind, rain, snow and storm conditions.

Does Keepie Yuppie have Game Center?

Yes. The game includes Game Center support.

Is Keepie Yuppie free?

Yes. It is free to download with in-app purchases.

What does Premium unlock?

Premium offers unlimited lives and no ads.
