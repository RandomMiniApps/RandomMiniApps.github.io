Keepie Yuppie

The details,
in plain English.

Features, intended use and limitations for Keepie Yuppie.

What Keepie Yuppie does

Keepie Yuppie turns simple tap-to-juggle gameplay into a 99-level challenge with physics, weather, obstacles and different ways to play.

The game is free to download, with a Premium option for unlimited lives and no ads.

Who is Keepie Yuppie for?

Keepie Yuppie is for players who enjoy quick skill games with physics and replayable challenges.

  • Casual arcade players
  • Fans of tap timing games
  • Players who like physics-based challenges
  • People who want short mobile game sessions
  • Game Center score chasers
  • Players who enjoy level progression and survival modes

Key features

99-level progression

Work through a long sequence of levels with increasing challenge.

Physics-based ball control

Tap to keep the ball airborne and react to motion, speed and position.

Dynamic weather

Wind, rain, snow and storm conditions change how the game feels.

Multiple modes

Play level challenges, survival mode and target practice.

How Keepie Yuppie works

  1. Launch the game and choose a level or mode.
  2. Tap to keep the ball airborne.
  3. Adapt to weather, obstacles and changing physics.
  4. Complete levels or chase a survival score.
  5. Use Game Center and Premium options if you want more competition or unlimited lives.

Useful for

Keepie Yuppie is useful for quick arcade sessions and skill-based challenges.

  • Timing practice
  • 99-level progression
  • Survival runs
  • Target practice
  • Weather challenges
  • Obstacle avoidance
  • Game Center competition
  • Short play sessions

Why use Keepie Yuppie?

Keepie Yuppie is built around immediate gameplay: tap, react and improve. The physics and weather keep each session from feeling identical.

Premium is optional and is intended for players who want unlimited lives and no ads.

Important notes

Keepie Yuppie is a casual game. Some features may require Game Center, ads or in-app purchases depending on how you choose to play.
