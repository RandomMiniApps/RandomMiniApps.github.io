About the app

What is Lighting Check?

Lighting Check is an iPhone and iPad app that estimates light intensity in lux using your device camera, compares practical guidance, and helps you document checks on device.

How do I measure light with Lighting Check?

Choose an environment, point the camera at the area, tap Start, and hold the device steady during the measurement.

Does Lighting Check use the rear camera?

Yes. The rear camera is used for best accuracy.

Are the readings as accurate as a professional lux meter?

No. Readings depend on your device camera and should be treated as estimates, not certified measurements. You can run a one-point calibration if you already have a trusted meter.

Can Lighting Check compare readings with standards?

Yes. It includes bundled environment guidance and lets you create custom standards on device.

Is Lighting Check free?

Yes. It is free to download with measurement credits, optional non-personalized rewarded ads for more credits, and a one-time Full Access purchase for unlimited measurements.
