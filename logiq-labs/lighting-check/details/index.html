Lighting Check

The details,
in plain English.

Features, intended use and limitations for Lighting Check.

What Lighting Check does

Lighting Check measures approximate light level in lux using your device camera. It is designed to help you understand whether a space is bright enough for common settings.

Choose bundled environment guidance or create custom standards, run a measurement, review the result, save audits on your device, and export PDF or CSV reports when you need to share or archive a check.

Who is Lighting Check for?

Lighting Check is for people who want a quick, camera-based light level estimate and light documentation workflow.

  • People checking room, workplace, or study lighting
  • Facility and farm location checks
  • People comparing spaces against practical lux guidance
  • Users who want on-device history, notes, and photos
  • Students learning about light levels
  • iPhone and iPad users who need a simple light measurement tool

Key features

Camera-based lux estimate

Use your device camera to sample light and estimate lux over a chosen duration.

Guidance and custom standards

Compare readings against bundled environments or standards you create on device.

Calibration

Run a one-point calibration against a trusted lux meter to improve consistency on your device.

History and exports

Save sites, rooms, and audits with notes and photos, then export PDF or CSV reports.

How Lighting Check works

  1. Choose the environment or custom standard you want to compare against.
  2. Point the rear camera at the area you want to measure.
  3. Tap Start and hold the device steady during sampling.
  4. Review the estimated lux, guidance comparison, and result details.
  5. Save the audit, add notes or photos, and export a PDF or CSV when needed.

Useful for

Lighting Check supports practical light checks and lightweight documentation.

  • Room light estimates
  • Workplace and facility checks
  • Farm or housing location checks
  • Target lux comparison
  • One-point calibration workflows
  • On-device audit history
  • PDF and CSV report sharing
  • Learning about light intensity

Why use Lighting Check?

Lighting Check gives a quick way to estimate lighting without dedicated hardware, which can be useful for informal checks, comparisons, and simple documentation.

Camera-based readings depend on device camera quality and conditions. Newer iPhones typically provide more consistent results. Calibration can improve consistency for your device, but it does not turn the phone into laboratory equipment.

Credits, ads, and Full Access

You start with free measurement credits. When credits run out, you can watch an optional rewarded ad when one is available, or unlock Full Access with a one-time In-App Purchase for unlimited measurements while the entitlement is active.

Rewarded ads are configured as non-personalized from the publisher side. Ad availability can vary, so rewarded credits are not guaranteed. Full Access does not erase your remaining credit balance.

Important notes

Lighting Check provides camera-based estimates and is not a replacement for calibrated professional measurement equipment.

Accuracy depends on device hardware, camera exposure, lighting conditions and how steadily the device is held.
