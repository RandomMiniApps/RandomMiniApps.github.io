How Dyslexia Reading Lens works

Explore the workflow before deciding whether the app is right for you.

  1. Bring your text

    Paste a passage, scan a page or import a supported document.

  2. Make it comfortable

    Adjust the font, spacing, colours and focus tools to suit your preferences.

  3. Read, listen and explore

    Use read-aloud highlighting and available AI tools to support comprehension. Check important details yourself.

There is no single right way to read. A little more space, a softer background, or a different font can make the page feel more comfortable. Take your time. Find the settings that feel right for you.

Try the settings · browser illustration

Your settings are personal

The browser example demonstrates text presentation using a sample paragraph. It does not process your documents or reproduce the full iPhone app. Display preferences are not a treatment for dyslexia.

In the app, AI tools depend on supported on-device capabilities. Generated explanations can be inaccurate; check important information.
