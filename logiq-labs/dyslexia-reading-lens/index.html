What is Dyslexia Reading Lens?

Dyslexia Reading Lens is a personal reading and comprehension workspace that combines comfortable text presentation, text-to-speech, scanning, and AI tools in one app.

Does Dyslexia Reading Lens cure or treat dyslexia?

No. It is not medical software. It supports reading comfort, listening, and comprehension but does not diagnose or treat dyslexia or any medical condition.

Where is my data stored?

Documents and reading preferences stay on your device. AI tools run on your device and do not send your document text to our servers. Subscriptions are handled by Apple.

Who is Dyslexia Reading Lens for?

It is designed for students and adults who find text visually overwhelming or tiring, including people with dyslexia, ADHD-related reading fatigue, visual stress, or language-learning needs.

Are AI responses always accurate?

No. AI simplify, summary, and word explanations may be wrong or incomplete. Verify important information, especially for coursework and work documents.

Is there a free tier?

Yes. The free tier includes paste, basic reading, limited scanning, text-to-speech, and a small number of AI actions. Pro subscriptions unlock higher limits.
