What is BoomColor?

BoomColor is a coloring book app for iPhone and iPad with a large gallery of outline pages, tap-to-fill painting, creative effects and daily challenges.

How do you color in BoomColor?

Choose a page, pick colors or effects, and tap regions to fill them. The app uses flood-fill painting with undo and redo support.

How many coloring pages are included?

The app includes 50 starter pages in the download plus hundreds more gallery pages delivered through Apple-hosted asset packs across ten browse categories.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. You can import photos from your library to use as coloring pages and save finished artwork back to Photos.

What are daily challenges?

Each day features a featured challenge page with goals for coverage, color and effects. Meeting all goals earns bonus credits in the app.

Is BoomColor free?

BoomColor is free to download. Credits, optional unlocks and ads support access to additional pages and creative tools.
