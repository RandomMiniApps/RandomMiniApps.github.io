Natural Text

The details,
in plain English.

Features, intended use and limitations for Natural Text.

What Natural Text does

Natural Text transforms messages into natural-looking text using your secret key. The result is designed to look less like broken ciphertext while still being reversible with the right key.

The app supports multiple languages and can be used alongside common messaging and sharing apps.

Who is Natural Text for?

Natural Text is for people who want a different style of text privacy and message transformation.

  • People who want natural-looking encrypted text
  • Users sharing transformed messages through chat apps
  • People who use email, SMS, WhatsApp or Telegram
  • Users who want a secret-key workflow
  • Learners interested in text transformation
  • iPhone and iPad users who want a focused privacy utility

Key features

Secret-key transformation

Encrypt and decrypt text using a secret key.

Natural-looking output

Generate output intended to look more like natural language than ordinary ciphertext.

Multi-language support

Use the app with support for multiple languages.

Share through other apps

Use transformed text in email, SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram and more.

How Natural Text works

  1. Enter your message.
  2. Enter your secret key.
  3. Choose whether to encrypt or decrypt.
  4. Copy the transformed text.
  5. Share or decode the text with the same key when needed.

Useful for

Natural Text supports privacy-minded text transformation and sharing workflows.

  • Secret-key text encryption
  • Natural-looking message output
  • Email sharing
  • SMS sharing
  • WhatsApp and Telegram workflows
  • Multiple language use
  • Encrypt/decrypt practice
  • Optional unlimited-character upgrade

Why use Natural Text?

Natural Text offers a distinctive way to transform messages before sharing them through ordinary communication apps.

It is best used as a personal privacy and text transformation tool. Do not rely on it as a replacement for audited security systems when safety-critical secrecy is required.

Important notes

Natural Text is a text transformation utility. Keep your secret key private and do not use the app as your only protection for safety-critical information.

Availability of features may depend on optional in-app purchases.
