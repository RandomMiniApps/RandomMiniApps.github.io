About the app

What is Natural Text?

Natural Text is an app that encrypts your text into natural-looking text using a secret key.

Where can I use the output?

You can use the transformed text in email, SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram and other apps that accept text.

Does Natural Text support multiple languages?

Yes. Natural Text supports multiple languages.

Do I need a secret key?

Yes. The secret key is used for encryption and decryption.

Is Natural Text free?

Yes. It is free with optional in-app purchases for unlimited characters and no ads.

Is Natural Text a replacement for audited security tools?

No. It is a privacy-minded text transformation app, not a replacement for audited secure communication systems.
