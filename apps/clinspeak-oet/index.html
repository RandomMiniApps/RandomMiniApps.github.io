Get product support ↗
What is ClinSpeak OET?

ClinSpeak OET is an independent OET-style speaking practice app for nurses.

Is ClinSpeak OET affiliated with OET?

No. ClinSpeak OET is not endorsed by or affiliated with the official OET test.

Does ClinSpeak OET provide an official score?

No. It provides practice feedback and does not claim to provide an official OET score.

Can I replay my answer?

Yes. The app supports transcript and audio replay after practice sessions.

Who is ClinSpeak OET for?

It is designed for nurses who want focused OET-style speaking practice.

Is clinical content medical advice?

No. Scenarios are fictional practice examples and are not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment instructions or professional clinical guidance.
