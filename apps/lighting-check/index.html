What Lighting Check does

Lighting Check measures approximate light level in lux using your device camera. It is designed to help you understand whether a space is bright enough for common settings.

Choose bundled environment guidance or create custom standards, run a measurement, review the result, save audits on your device, and export PDF or CSV reports when you need to share or archive a check.

How Lighting Check works

  1. Choose the environment or custom standard you want to compare against.
  2. Point the rear camera at the area you want to measure.
  3. Tap Start and hold the device steady during sampling.
  4. Review the estimated lux, guidance comparison, and result details.
  5. Save the audit, add notes or photos, and export a PDF or CSV when needed.

Why use Lighting Check?

Lighting Check gives a quick way to estimate lighting without dedicated hardware, which can be useful for informal checks, comparisons, and simple documentation.

Camera-based readings depend on device camera quality and conditions. Newer iPhones typically provide more consistent results. Calibration can improve consistency for your device, but it does not turn the phone into laboratory equipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lighting Check?

Lighting Check is an iPhone and iPad app that estimates light intensity in lux using your device camera, compares practical guidance, and helps you document checks on device.

How do I measure light with Lighting Check?

Choose an environment, point the camera at the area, tap Start, and hold the device steady during the measurement.

Does Lighting Check use the rear camera?

Yes. The rear camera is used for best accuracy.

Are the readings as accurate as a professional lux meter?

No. Readings depend on your device camera and should be treated as estimates, not certified measurements. You can run a one-point calibration if you already have a trusted meter.

Can Lighting Check compare readings with standards?

Yes. It includes bundled environment guidance and lets you create custom standards on device.

Is Lighting Check free?

Yes. It is free to download with measurement credits, optional non-personalized rewarded ads for more credits, and a one-time Full Access purchase for unlimited measurements.

Does Lighting Check track me for ads?

Lighting Check is configured for non-personalized rewarded ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency. Regional advertising consent may still appear through Google’s User Messaging Platform where required. See the Privacy Policy.

Important notes

Lighting Check provides camera-based estimates and is not a replacement for calibrated professional measurement equipment.

Accuracy depends on device hardware, camera exposure, lighting conditions and how steadily the device is held.
