HotspotCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

HotspotCheck (“the app”) is an educational Wi-Fi safety advisor. It checks visible safety indicators on the network you are connected to. It is not a security guarantee or professional audit service.

Who we are

The app is published by Random Mini Apps. See the developer website for support.

Data processed on your device

  • Wi-Fi name and security details: Shown where iOS allows, typically after you grant location permission while using the app.
  • Local network discovery: When you start a scan, the app may discover visible devices and services on the Wi-Fi you are using (Bonjour/mDNS and read-only UPnP/SSDP discovery).
  • DNS and connectivity probes: User-initiated checks against well-known test endpoints to assess reachability and visible risk indicators.
  • Scan reports: Stored locally on your iPhone when you enable history. You can delete reports from Settings.

What we do not do

  • No analytics SDK or third-party advertising.
  • No developer-operated backend for scan data.
  • No upload of SSIDs, IP addresses, device names, or scan history to our servers.
  • No hidden or background network scanning.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details.

Exports

If you export or share a report, you choose where that file goes. Exported content is under your control.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences learning about Wi-Fi safety. Users who need parental consent in their region should use the app with a guardian as appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support link in the App Store listing or the developer website.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
