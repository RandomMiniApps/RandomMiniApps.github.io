Phase 8C

Doctor quick start (phone)

After unlock, review images and annotations stay on this device and are not uploaded by this application.

  1. Open the review website on your phone.
  2. Tap Load my package.
  3. Choose your assigned ID (doctor01–doctor05).
  4. Choose Part A and enter the password you were given.
  5. Wait for download + unlock (~40–50 MB).
  6. For each case: view → tap through the wizard → Save & next.
  7. Export a progress backup whenever you pause. To restore it, load the same part and tap Restore a progress backup.
  8. You can use Review or correct any case before final export.
  9. At 248/248, export the final feedback ZIP.
  10. Repeat for Part B (248 different cases) using the same password.
  11. Return both final ZIPs through the approved channel.

Rules

  • Do not assess fracture status.
  • Do not share your password or open another doctor’s package.
  • Do not try to guess dataset source or model output.
  • Part A and Part B do not repeat cases for you. Every case is independently reviewed by another doctor.

This tool is research software, not a certified medical device.
